How VAT works

When you record an expense or confirm a booking:

  • If VAT is ON, the system automatically calculates the tax portion.
  • If VAT is OFF, the full amount is recorded as the net total.

This ensures your Profit & Loss statements remain accurate regardless of your tax jurisdiction.

Changing the rate

Past ledger entries will not be recalculated if you change the VAT rate. The rate at the time of recording is permanently attached to that entry.
