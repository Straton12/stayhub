What happens next?
  1. You submit the listing.
  2. Our team reviews it (usually within 24 hours).
  3. Once approved, guests can book directly.
Tips for a great listing
  • Use a real photo for the cover.
  • Write the short description as the first 10 seconds of a conversation.
  • List at least 4 amenities.
  • Be specific about the location.
  • Add WhatsApp if you want guests to reach you instantly.
Availability

Bookings lock the listing automatically for the dates chosen. When a booking ends, the listing reopens on its own — you don't need to do anything.
